Overview

What is InSight?

InSight bridges a critical gap in Braille education: the absence of a low-cost device to measure fluency while keeping students on paper Braille.

📖

Fluency Measurement

InSight tracks the time a student spends on each character of Braille, calculating instantaneous reading speed and identifying regressions in real time. No existing low-cost tool does this on real paper Braille.

🔌

Simple Hardware

An app run just using a phone camera, with no expensive sensors or hardware parts. All users need is a phone, a passage, and a tripod.

📊

Instant Data for Teachers

The code converts readings into a CSV file, and a color-coded heat map overlaid on the Braille passage (showing speed and regressions), giving teachers immediate insight into their students' reading patterns and areas for improvement.

The Problem

Braille learners lack the tools they deserve.

Fluency (reading speed, accuracy, and expression) is fundamental for effective understanding of text. But Braille lacks a direct equivalent to tools that help evaluate visual reading fluency, making it difficult for teachers to evaluate the nuanced aspects of tactile reading.

While tools like refreshable Braille displays and screen readers exist, they are often expensive, and move students away from the optimal experience of paper Braille.

The Versions

Three iterations. One goal.

InSight evolved through three distinct versions, each shaped by real student testing and a push for richer reading insight.

Version 1
The Line Reader
ESP-32 + VL53L1X Time-of-Flight sensor with a line blocker and start button. Measures per-line reading speed and uploads data to Google Sheets.
ESP-32 ToF Sensor

Version 2
Refined Design
Informed by testing at the California School for the Blind. Removed the line blocker; added a pressure sensor and non-slip mat.
