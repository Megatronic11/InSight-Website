The App

A phone camera app that overlays a color-coded speed heat map on the student's Braille text — showing exactly where they slow down, and flagging every regression.

Overview

From hardware to software

Version 3 is a complete rethink: instead of a physical device, it runs entirely as an app on the student's phone camera. By tracking reading in real time through computer vision, it captures far more data than any hardware approach could.

The result is character-by-character and section-by-section feedback — displayed as a color-coded speed heat map overlaid directly onto an image of the text. Teachers see not just how fast a student reads a line, but exactly which words or sections need targeted instruction.

Key Features

What V3 can do that V1 & V2 couldn't

The shift to software unlocks a whole new class of insight — moving from line-level timing to a full picture of how a student reads.

🌡
Color-Coded Speed Heat Map

A live overlay on the text image shows reading speed at the character, word, or line level. Slow sections appear in red; faster sections in green — giving an instant visual picture of the reading session.

↩
Regression Detection

The app flags when a student re-reads a word or section — a key comprehension signal. Regressions are marked directly on the heat map so teachers can spot recurring patterns at a glance.

🔍
Character-Level Granularity

Unlike V1 and V2, which only captured whole-line times, V3 tracks reading at the character or word level — revealing micro-level patterns that line averages can't show.

📈
Line-by-Line Speed Summary

Teachers still get the familiar line-by-line reading speed output from V1 and V2 — but now with section and character detail layered on top for a complete picture.

🎓
Targeted Instruction

By identifying exactly which sections a student struggles with, teachers can focus instruction where it matters most rather than relying on overall speed averages alone.

📷
Phone Camera Only

No physical hardware required. V3 runs on a standard smartphone, making it accessible to any classroom without specialized equipment or 3D-printed parts.

For Teachers

A high-fidelity reading comprehension assessment

Think of V3 as a high-fidelity Braille reading level test — similar in depth to the assessments used for sighted students, but designed specifically for tactile readers. A teacher reviewing a V3 session can see:

1
Line-by-Line Reading Speeds
The same fluency metric as V1 and V2 — how many seconds each line took, and the student's average reading speed across the full passage.
2
Heat Map Overlay on the Text
A color-coded view of the exact Braille text showing which words or characters took the longest — teachers can pinpoint specific cells or contractions causing difficulty.
3
Regression Markers
Every re-read is flagged on the map. Patterns of regression — like repeatedly going back over a specific contraction — surface immediately and can inform the next lesson.
💡

Why this matters

For sighted readers, tools like DIBELS and AIMSweb give teachers granular reading data. Braille readers haven't had an equivalent — until now. V3 brings the same level of diagnostic insight to Braille instruction, enabling targeted, data-driven teaching.
